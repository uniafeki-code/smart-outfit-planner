How to make everyday outfits easier

Start with the practical constraint that matters most: weather and what the day requires. Then choose one direction for the look — relaxed, polished, feminine, confident or minimal. A useful outfit usually needs only four decisions: a top, a bottom, shoes and the right layer.

Repeatable formulas are not boring. They are what make a wardrobe useful. A blouse, straight jeans, loafers and a trench can become more polished or more casual with small changes. The same principle works for workdays, travel, dinners and weekends.

“The goal is not a perfect outfit. It is a clear next decision.”
